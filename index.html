About this map

Source: the Washington County Sheriff's Office weekly media incident summaries (MediaReports/RMS), every weekly file the county publishes (209 files, Sep 4, 2022 – Sep 5, 2026), de-duplicated by case number. The log covers the Sheriff's Office and the city police departments that share its records system (Oakdale, Woodbury, Stillwater, Cottage Grove, Bayport, Forest Lake, St. Paul Park). Coverage before 2023 is partial.

Positions are approximate by design. The county publishes addresses at block level ("5XXX 157th St N") or as intersections. Each dot is placed on the named street using OpenStreetMap address points where they exist, otherwise Washington County's house-number grid (about 1,000 numbers per mile), then nudged a few dozen meters so overlapping calls stay visible. Intersections are computed from OpenStreetMap centerlines. Older river towns (Stillwater, Bayport, Mahtomedi, Newport…) use their own numbering, so more of their calls land at street level. Incidents with no usable address are counted but not drawn.

Severity tiers are an editorial grouping of the county's 1,320 event labels, not an official ranking. Base map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
